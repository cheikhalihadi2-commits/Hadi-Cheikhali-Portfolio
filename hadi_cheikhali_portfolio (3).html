Your hook is doing 80% of the work. Nobody reads a post with a weak first line. Here's the 3-part formula I use for every piece of content I write: 1. Tension — state something that feels wrong or surprising 2. Specificity — add a number, name, or timeframe 3. Promise — make it clear what they get if they keep reading Example: "I wrote 400 LinkedIn posts last year. Only 12 drove real business. Here's what was different."
